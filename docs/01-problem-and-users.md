# Problem and User needs
## The problem
Students at Uganda Christian University report lost and Found property in whatapp groups and messages get covered
The owner may never see them.There is no way to check who really owns the Item and nobody records when it is returned

## Users and their needs
### student who lost an item
- Search for an item by keyword, place and date
- Claim it privately without exposing details publicly

### student who found an item
- Reports the Item with place, date and details private
- Checks for claims
### Administrator.
- Checks for claims  and record decisions
- Verify claim Authentication
